01 / About

A short introduction

I'm a recent Computer Science grad from California State University, Fullerton who gravitates toward the parts of software most people skip past.

My core is backend engineering — designing APIs that don't fall over, modeling data so future-me doesn't curse past-me, and stitching services together with authentication, queues, and sane error budgets. Lately I've been pulling that discipline into applied AI: training computer vision models, wiring them to production endpoints, and shipping the unglamorous infrastructure around them.

Outside of code I'm reading about systems design, watching markets do silly things, and quietly building side projects. If you're working on something ambitious — especially in fintech, data tooling, or AI infrastructure — I'd love to talk.
